# Industry Benchmarks — Reference List

Per directive §5. Re-verify against current official docs before each feature; product
capability moves. Anything unverified is marked.

## Band
- Free team-communication app; widely used by band and athletic programs.
- **NFHS "official and exclusive" team communication partner.**
- iOS, Android, web. Chat, schedule, photos, polls, notifications.
- Publishes a DPA process for districts.
- Absent: rosters with eligibility, verified stats, scoring, film, compliance.
- Lesson: reach (Android + web + free + district paperwork) is the moat, not features.

## TeamSnap / TeamSnap ONE
- Strong family model: one guardian, multiple athletes, per-athlete RSVP. *(verify current)*
- Registration, fees, availability, messaging, schedule.
- Youth/club orientation; school compliance is shallow.
- Lesson to copy: the multi-athlete switch without re-login.

## SportsEngine HQ
- Org websites, registration, tournaments, member management; strong at club scale.
- DPA and onboarding processes are mature.
- Admin-heavy UX; parent experience is thinner.
- Lesson to avoid: an admin console pretending to be a product.

## GameChanger
- Live scoring families genuinely follow; polished scorekeeping UX.
- Score-first: no AD layer, no compliance, no cross-sport records.
- Lesson: live game data is the emotional hook. RockLab already has play-by-play and a
  spectator view — that is the differentiator to lean on.

## Stack Team App
- Simple team comms, schedule, media.
- Generic; no school records or compliance.
- Lesson: simplicity sells, but has no switching reason for a school.
